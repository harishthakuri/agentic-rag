Agent mode: the AI plans its own searches

Instead of searching once with your words, the chat AI decides what to search for and can search several times, for example once per part of a two-part question, before writing the answer. Each search is shown live.

Asking vs. uploading

Uploading is handled in the background by a worker, and nobody waits for it. Asking is handled by the API server itself, because you are waiting for the answer. The same fingerprint model is used in both, so questions and passages can be compared.

Three AI moments, two models

  1. The translator (step 3, qwen3-embedding) turns your question into a meaning fingerprint.
  2. The reviewer (step 5, gpt-oss) reads each candidate passage and grades whether it answers the question.
  3. The writer (step 8, gpt-oss) writes the answer, citing the passages it used.

Guards against made-up answers

  • The writer may use only the numbered passages, not its general knowledge.
  • No relevant passages means no AI answer, just an honest “couldn’t find it”.
  • Every citation is checked against the passages that were provided.
  • Instructions hidden inside documents are ignored: passages are treated as data, not commands.

Ask or Agent?

Ask: one search with your words, so it is quick and predictable. Best for direct questions.
Agent: the AI plans its own searches. Best for questions with several parts.
