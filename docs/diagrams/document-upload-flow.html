Why is AI used here, and only here?

Fingerprints let the system find passages by meaning, not just matching words. Later, a question like “how do I stop browsers using old JavaScript?” finds the section on “cache busting”, even though they share almost no words. This is the only AI step during upload. The chat AI that writes answers is not involved until someone asks a question.

Where AI is involved

One AI model, at one step (9). It works like a librarian who files every page under the ideas it contains, not just the words it uses. That's why a question can find the right passage even when it is phrased differently.

The chat AI that writes answers, with references to these passages, is only used later, when someone asks a question.

Safe and reliable by design

  • Access keys are required; only a scrambled form of each key is stored.
  • Files are checked before anything is stored; duplicates are refused.
  • Temporary failures are retried automatically (up to 3 attempts); for anything else, the reason is shown in the app.
  • The AI models run locally: no document leaves our machine.

Glossary

API server
The “front desk” that receives and checks every request.
Background worker
A helper that processes jobs from the to-do list.
Passage
A short piece of a document, about half a page.
Meaning fingerprint
Numbers that capture what a passage is about (technically, an “embedding”).
Database
PostgreSQL with pgvector: stores text, fingerprints and the word index in one place.
